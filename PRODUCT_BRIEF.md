# Kelvar LLC Atmospheric Water Bottle — Preliminary Product Brief

**Status:** Concept / feasibility stage  
**Purpose:** Partner, engineering and prototype discussions  
**Product category:** Portable atmospheric water generation and collection

## Concept
A personal bottle-format device that draws ambient air across a cooled surface. When that surface is below the air's dew point, water vapor may condense and be collected. A future design could incorporate water-quality treatment, sensors, a reservoir and controlled dispensing.

## Proposed subsystems
- Air intake, washable particulate screen and fan
- Cooling module and heat exchanger
- Condensate channel and covered collection reservoir
- Treatment stages selected after water-quality risk assessment
- Humidity, temperature and reservoir-level sensors
- Battery management, charging and thermal / electrical protection
- Cleanable enclosure and documented food-contact wetted materials

## Critical feasibility questions
1. Can the required water yield be achieved at a useful size?
2. What is the energy cost per liter under realistic conditions?
3. How will heat be rejected without making the bottle uncomfortable?
4. How will the water path be kept clean, dryable and resistant to biofilm?
5. Which treatment stages are necessary based on independent condensate testing?
6. What is the device's actual operating envelope by humidity and temperature?
7. Can the bill of materials and assembly process support a viable product?

## Proposed validation protocol
Record ambient temperature and relative humidity, inlet and cold-surface temperatures, water mass collected, elapsed time, fan and cooling power, total watt-hours, noise and thermal behavior. Repeat tests at multiple controlled climate points and report results with test conditions. Have collected water assessed by a qualified laboratory before describing it as potable.

## Partner roles sought
- Thermodynamics / HVAC / atmospheric water generation engineering
- Industrial and mechanical design
- Thermal management and battery systems
- Water treatment, microbiology and analytical testing
- Prototype fabrication and manufacturing engineering
- University or laboratory test facilities
- Pilot customers and strategic funding partners

## Claims policy
Until validated, do not advertise a specific production rate, cycle time, purification guarantee, health benefit, cost, environmental savings, certification, patent status or commercial availability. Condensation alone does not establish drinking-water safety. Water quality depends on air contaminants, wetted materials, storage conditions and validated treatment.

## Development sequence
Feasibility modeling → instrumented bench prototype → independent water and safety testing → integrated industrial design → pilot build → regulatory and manufacturing readiness.
