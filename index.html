The opportunity

Personal hydration,
reimagined as a platform.

The concept brings atmospheric water generation into a familiar, portable form. Rather than relying on a conventional refill alone, the device is intended to explore on-demand water production wherever humidity and available power make it practical.

◉

Portable by design

A compact, reusable bottle format intended for daily routines, travel and field use, with a modular lid and serviceable internal components.

⌁

Built around the air

A controlled airflow path and cooled condensation surface aim to collect water vapor when ambient conditions support efficient condensation.

✧

Designed for iteration

Replaceable filters, accessible components and measurable performance targets make the concept suitable for prototyping and partner-led development.

Technology overview

From humidity to collected water.

The proposed architecture uses established atmospheric-water-generation principles in a miniaturized system. The engineering challenge is balancing water yield, power consumption, size, noise, temperature and safe materials.

1

Draw in ambient air

A low-voltage fan moves surrounding air through an intake screen and particulate pre-filter.

2

Cool below dew point

A thermoelectric or other compact cooling module chills a heat-exchanger surface so moisture can condense into droplets.

3

Collect and condition

A sealed, drainable reservoir collects condensate. A validated treatment train would address particulates, taste, microbial risks and material compatibility.

4

Monitor and dispense

Sensors and controls monitor temperature, humidity, tank level and operating status before water is made available to the user.

Important engineering reality: Atmospheric water output depends strongly on relative humidity and air temperature. Cooling air takes energy, and a small bottle-sized device may produce water slowly or inefficiently in dry or cool conditions. No production rate, purification claim or drinking-water certification is being represented as proven at this stage.

PowerRechargeable battery, protected charging circuit and power management
Water pathFood-contact condensate channel, covered reservoir and dispensing valve
TreatmentValidated filtration and disinfection stages selected through water testing
ControlsTemperature / humidity sensing, level detection, status indicators and safety cutoffs
EnclosureDurable, cleanable housing with documented food-contact materials
Design priorities

Engineered for trust.

Water safetyPrevent stagnant water, manage biofilm risk, validate treatment and test finished water in an accredited lab.
Thermal designReject heat efficiently while protecting the user from hot surfaces and condensation leaks.
Battery safetyUse certified cells, battery management, over-current and thermal protection, and compliant charging.
CleanabilityDesign for disassembly, drying, routine sanitation and replacement of wetted components.
Honest metricsMeasure liters per day, watt-hours per liter, noise, operating range and cost across climate conditions.

Development roadmap

Prove the fundamentals.
Then scale the product.

Kelvar LLC is seeking collaborators to turn the concept into a testable, manufacturable product through staged engineering and evidence-based decisions.

Phase 1Feasibility
Model dew-point conditions, energy budget, expected yield, component size and target use environments.
Phase 2Bench prototype
Build an instrumented air-to-water module and record output, power draw, temperature, humidity and condensate quality.
Phase 3Safety & validation
Evaluate materials, microbiological controls, cleaning, battery safety, reliability and independent water testing.
Phase 4Product pilot
Refine enclosure, usability, serviceability, supplier costs and small-batch manufacturing with pilot partners.
